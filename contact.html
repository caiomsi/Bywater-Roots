Good questions

Frequently asked questions

Short answers to what neighbors ask most. Don't see yours? Use the form above.

Do I need gardening experience?

Not at all. Plenty of our neighbors will be planting for the very first time. Volunteers and seasoned gardeners will be around on Saturdays, and our winter workshops cover the basics, including Utah frost dates and what to plant when.

Is water provided?

Yes. Water and shared tools are included with every plot, so you only need to bring yourself and maybe a hat. We'll set up a watering rotation with volunteers to keep things happy during Utah's hot, dry summers.

Can kids participate?

Absolutely. The kids' learning plot sits right next to the playground, and Kids' Garden Club meets on Wednesdays from June to August for ages 5–12. Children are welcome in the garden with an adult, and families can share a plot.

What does a plot cost, and are scholarships available?

Plots are per season: $30 for a 4×8 bed, $40 for a 4×12 bed and $30 for an ADA-height accessible bed. Scholarships are available on a pay-what-you-can basis, no questions asked. Water and shared tools are always included.

Are there accessible beds?

Yes. We plan ADA-height raised beds near the path and parking, with priority for gardeners who need them. They cost the same as a standard 4×8 bed, and our paths are designed to be wheelchair-friendly.

What happens in winter?

The garden rests from November to March, but we don't. Winter workshops run at the Cottonwood Heights Recreation Center, like seed-starting in February and garden planning in March, so you're ready when the beds open in April.
