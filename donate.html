Donate

Help us plant the first row.

Every gift would turn a grassy edge of Bywater Park into beds, tools and a place for kids to dig. Here's what a donation does.

Why it matters

A small gift goes a long way

Beds for everyone

Donations keep plot prices low and fund scholarships, so a neighbor on a tight budget can still have a bed, including ADA-height ones.

Kids who dig

Seeds, small tools and weekly lessons keep the kids' plot beside the playground running for children ages 5–12.

A garden that lasts

Compost, native flowers and good soil mean the garden gets healthier every season instead of starting over.
