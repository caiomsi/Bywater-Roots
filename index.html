A community garden for Bywater Park

Grow together, right down the street.

Bywater Roots is a proposed neighborhood garden on a quiet grassy edge of Bywater Park in Cottonwood Heights: raised beds, a kids' learning plot, native flowers and a table to gather around.

Our idea

What is Bywater Roots?

Bywater Park already has a playground, ball fields, a pavilion and plenty of neighbors passing through. What it doesn't have yet is a place to grow things together. We imagine turning an underused strip of grass into a garden anyone nearby can join.

Whether you're a family with kids, a retiree who misses digging in the dirt, or a neighbor without a yard, there would be a bed, a tool and a friendly face waiting. Plots would run April through October, with winter workshops at the Recreation Center.
