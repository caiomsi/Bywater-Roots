Partner with us

Schools, scouts and local businesses

Three ideas to start the conversation. We're happy to shape something that fits your group.

Field trips & class plots

Bring a class to the kids' plot beside the playground, or adopt a bed your students plant in spring and harvest in fall.

Scout service projects

Troops can take on a build-day crew, a pollinator-strip planting or a compost station for a badge or a service-hours project.

Business sponsorship

Sponsor a bed with a name plaque, supply tools or soil, or host a build-day lunch. See our sponsor wall.
